# Fix for sourceloom.me - Blog URLs Not Inspectable

**Good news:** Your domain verification works (https://sourceloom.me is verified ✅)

**Issue:** Blog URLs not yet in Google's index

**Solution:** Submit your sitemap and blog URLs explicitly

---

## Why This Happens

When you first verify a domain, Google hasn't crawled the blog URLs yet. They're there, but Google doesn't know about them until you:
1. Submit the sitemap (tells Google where all URLs are)
2. Explicitly request indexing for specific blog URLs

---

## FIX THIS NOW (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Submit Sitemap
In Google Search Console (you should already be here):

1. Click **Sitemaps** (left menu, under "Index")
2. In the "Add a new sitemap" box, type: `sitemap.xml`
3. Click **Submit**
4. Wait 10 seconds - status should show **"Success"** ✅

### Step 2: Request Indexing for Blog URLs
1. At the TOP of Google Search Console, click the **URL inspection** search box
2. Paste this exact URL:
   ```
   https://sourceloom.me/blog/how-to-choose-bangladesh-apparel-manufacturer
   ```
3. Press Enter
4. Wait for the report (may say "URL is not on Google" - that's normal)
5. Click the blue **"Request indexing"** button
6. A popup says "Indexing requested"

**Repeat for 2 more key posts:**
- `https://sourceloom.me/blog/why-source-apparel-bangladesh-not-domestic`
- `https://sourceloom.me/blog/european-apparel-sourcing-compliance-guide`

### Step 3: Wait
- Google will crawl within **24-48 hours**
- You can then inspect blog URLs freely
- Results may not show for 2-4 weeks
